# Week 2 – GPS Mobility and Spatial Analysis

## 1. Objective
The objective of Week 2 was to analyze GPS mobility patterns and identify spatial activity patterns using GPS pings, sensor locations, and pedestrian count data.

## 2. GPS Mobility Analysis
- Analyzed 50,000 GPS records.
- Calculated hourly mobility patterns.
- Analyzed device-level mobility and estimated movement speed.
- Identified the top 10 most active devices.

### Generated Reports
- `hourly_mobility.csv`
- `device_mobility.csv`
- `mobility_summary.csv`

### Visualizations

## 3. Spatial Analysis
- Analyzed 50,000 GPS records.
- Used 91 sensor location records.
- Analyzed 10,000 pedestrian count records.
- Calculated GPS latitude and longitude ranges.
- Aggregated pedestrian counts by sensor.
- Identified the top 10 busiest sensor locations.
- Generated 3,088 GPS spatial density grids.

### Key Finding
Flinders St–Elizabeth St (East) was the busiest sensor location, with a total pedestrian count of 289,217.
